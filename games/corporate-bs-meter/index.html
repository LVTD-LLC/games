The plain-English version.

Playing needs no name or email. A game-only cookie remembers your player for 90 days; this is not an account and does not verify your identity.

Your phrase is sent to TypeSafe’s Jev to judge it. Every scored phrase is saved with a shareable page. Your best score and phrase enter the global ranking, using your name or Anonymous if you leave it blank. The top ten appear on the leaderboard. Clearing your name changes your public attribution to Anonymous; it does not remove your results.

Your optional email is stored separately, only when you choose new-game updates. It is never public or sent to the AI. Every game-update email will include an unsubscribe link.

We keep scores to run the game. Temporary hashed network counters limit abuse; raw IP addresses are not stored in the game database.

Do not submit confidential information or other people’s personal details. This is a comedy game, not a claim about anyone’s honesty.

We use anonymous PostHog analytics across our games for visits, game actions and errors. Names, emails and phrases are excluded.
