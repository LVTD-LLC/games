        How it works

        Jev is TypeSafe’s decision model, not a traditional chess engine. On every turn, it sees the board and every legal move. It gives each option a probability; the highest-probability move gets played. No hidden Stockfish, no random fallback.

        All normal chess moves are supported, including castling, en passant and your choice of promotion. Checkmate wins; stalemate, repetition, the 50-move rule and insufficient material end in a draw. A 300-move session limit keeps games bounded.

        Your board is saved in this browser. New games also keep a verified move history on the server so completed results can be ranked. Winners, move counts and optional names are public. Emails are private and only collected with your opt-in. Only the chess position and move history are sent to Jev. No account needed.
